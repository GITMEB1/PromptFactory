# Note Hygiene Policy

## Scope
Applies to intermediate research notes, claim inventories, and synthesis artifacts used before final response generation.

## Required behaviors
- Separate raw excerpts, interpretations, and decisions into distinct sections.
- Timestamp notes for volatile topics and mark stale notes for review.
- Remove duplicate claims or merge them with canonical identifiers.
- Keep notes concise, scannable, and structured for downstream audit.
- Mark unresolved questions explicitly rather than burying them in prose.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Mixing copied source text with original analysis without labeling.
- Keeping contradictory notes without conflict flags.
- Carrying forward stale assumptions as current facts.
- Storing unverifiable claims without a validation status.

## Exceptions
- Quick exploratory notes may be rough, but must be cleaned before being used for final outputs.
- Personal shorthand is allowed if traceability fields remain intact.
- In emergency/time-critical workflows, deferred cleanup is acceptable with explicit follow-up marker.

## Audit checks
- Notes use clear labels for source excerpt vs interpretation.
- Duplicate/contradictory entries are reconciled or flagged.
- Staleness markers exist for time-sensitive topics.
- Unresolved items are explicitly listed and tracked.
