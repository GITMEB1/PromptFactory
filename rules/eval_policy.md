# Evaluation Policy

## Scope
Applies to post-run quality evaluation for substantive outputs, especially high-stakes or externally shared deliverables.

## Required behaviors
- Evaluate each run across at least: source routing, freshness, credibility, conflict handling, grounding, clarity, usefulness, proportionality, and brevity discipline.
- Use explicit pass/warn/fail judgments with short justifications per dimension.
- Escalate any fail in high-risk dimensions (grounding, freshness, conflict handling) before release.
- Track recurrent warning patterns to inform prompt/process updates.
- Include a final overall confidence score aligned with dimension-level outcomes.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Shipping high-risk outputs without completing evaluation.
- Passing runs based only on style quality while factual checks fail.
- Ignoring repeated warning trends across runs.
- Treating evaluator uncertainty as pass-by-default.

## Exceptions
- Very low-stakes drafts may use lightweight evaluation (minimum grounding + clarity checks).
- Time-critical workflows may defer deep stylistic review, but not factual safety checks.
- Internal ideation outputs can skip external-readiness checks if clearly labeled draft.

## Audit checks
- Evaluation record exists and covers all required dimensions.
- Any fail has remediation action or explicit release block.
- Overall confidence is consistent with dimension ratings.
- Repeated warning categories are logged for process improvement.
