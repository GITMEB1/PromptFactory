# Revision Policy

## Scope
Applies to iterative updates after draft generation, including factual corrections, restructuring, compression, and tone adjustments.

## Required behaviors
- Revalidate factual claims after substantive revisions.
- Preserve or improve citation coverage through every revision pass.
- Track material changes (what changed and why) for high-stakes outputs.
- Re-run critical lint checks before final release.
- Ensure revised conclusions remain supported by evidence, not just better prose.

## Prohibited behaviors
- Editing that removes evidence or caveats without replacement.
- Introducing new claims during rewrite without sourcing.
- "Stealth" meaning shifts caused by aggressive summarization.
- Treating grammar/style fixes as exempt from factual integrity checks when content changed.

## Exceptions
- Pure formatting edits with no semantic changes may skip full revalidation.
- Minor typo corrections do not require change logs.
- Emergency corrections may be fast-tracked, but must be followed by full validation pass.

## Audit checks
- Post-revision claim inventory still aligns with citations.
- Material revisions have rationale notes.
- Critical lint checks pass after final edit.
- No unsupported claims are introduced during revision.
