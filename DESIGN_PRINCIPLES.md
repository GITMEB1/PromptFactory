# Design Principles

## 1. Evidence over eloquence
Good phrasing cannot substitute for support quality.

## 2. Explicit source-class routing
Every material claim should have a declared source path.

## 3. Freshness-aware reasoning
Recency-sensitive questions require live or recently verified sources.

## 4. Conflict transparency
When sources disagree, present divergence rather than force synthesis.

## 5. Risk-proportional depth
Assurance activities scale with task risk and consequence.

## 6. Human-accountable escalation
High-stakes ambiguity and unresolved conflicts require human review.

## 7. Reproducibility and traceability
Workflow artifacts should allow replay, audit, and post-run improvement.
