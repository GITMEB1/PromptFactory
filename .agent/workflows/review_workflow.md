# Review Workflow

## When to use
Use when validating existing outputs for correctness, completeness, compliance, or quality.

## Inputs
- Artifact(s) under review
- Review rubric or acceptance criteria
- Baseline expectations (policy, style, tests)

## Sequence
1. Confirm review scope and quality bar.
2. Inspect artifacts against criteria systematically.
3. Record findings by severity (blocking, major, minor, suggestion).
4. Verify fixes or provide actionable remediation guidance.
5. Produce final review decision and residual risk note.

## Outputs
- Structured review report
- Defect list with severity and suggested fixes
- Go/no-go recommendation

## Failure conditions
- Acceptance criteria are undefined.
- Artifact set is incomplete for meaningful review.
- Required validation tools/checks are unavailable.

## Handoff
Share decision status, prioritized findings, and minimal next actions to reach approval.
