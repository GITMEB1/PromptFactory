# Research Workflow

## When to use
Use when source acquisition and context-building are materially distinct from normal implementation flow.

## Inputs
- Research question(s) or uncertainty list
- Candidate source locations (repo docs, specs, external references)
- Decision deadline or depth expectations
- Minimum credibility/freshness threshold

## Sequence
1. Define research objectives and evidence quality bar.
2. Gather sources prioritized by authority and relevance.
3. Extract key findings and map them to task decisions.
4. Identify unresolved gaps and confidence level per finding.
5. Produce a recommendation package for execution.

## Outputs
- Structured findings summary
- Source-backed recommendations
- Explicit unknowns and assumptions
- Suggested downstream mode/workflow

## Failure conditions
- No sufficiently credible sources found.
- Findings remain contradictory without a resolution path.
- Time constraints prevent meeting required confidence.

## Handoff contract
Provide all of the following before handing back to `default_workflow.md`:
1. **Question ledger:** each question marked resolved/partial/open.
2. **Source ledger:** source, authority rationale, and freshness note.
3. **Decision mapping:** which finding informs which task decision.
4. **Confidence profile:** high/medium/low with why.
5. **Recommended next mode:** implementation, analysis, writing, review, or source-conflict, with trigger reason.
