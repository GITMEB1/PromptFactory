# Glossary

- **Claim control**: Governance of material claims through inclusion, exclusion, revision, and approval states before drafting.
- **Claim inventory**: Structured list of assertions the output depends on, including support status and disposition.
- **Composition**: Assembly of approved claims into a final response without introducing unsupported new facts.
- **Context engineering**: Deliberate structuring of task context, evidence, and workflow artifacts to maximize relevance and control.
- **Decision gate**: Mandatory checkpoint that must pass before continuing.
- **Deep mode**: Assurance-focused workflow variant with stronger evidence checks.
- **Escalation**: Transfer to a stricter mode or human reviewer due to risk/uncertainty.
- **Evaluation**: Post-draft pass/warn/fail assessment across factuality, policy, and utility dimensions.
- **Freshness requirement**: Expected recency tolerance for supporting evidence.
- **Grounding**: Explicit linkage between claims and supporting evidence, with uncertainty disclosures where support is partial.
- **Lessons**: Reusable run-level learnings captured in tracking artifacts for future process and prompt improvements.
- **Lightweight mode**: Speed-focused workflow variant for low-risk tasks.
- **Material claim**: Assertion that could change decisions if wrong.
- **Model prior**: Information produced from model prior knowledge absent current retrieval.
- **Prompt engineering**: Design of prompt/module instructions to produce reliable, auditable behavior.
- **Retrieval**: Evidence acquisition from external or indexed sources to satisfy freshness and coverage needs.
- **Source class**: Category of evidence origin (official, practitioner, private, retrieval, prior).
- **Source routing**: Policy-guided assignment of claims to source classes based on risk, freshness, and authority.
